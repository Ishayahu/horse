# Author-first research protocol — 2026-11-25
Status: user-approved direction, Christian exegesis only. Supersedes episode-first research order; does not supersede original research question or existing source evidence. No change to global research queue.
## Goal
Reconstruct each author's coherent (or demonstrably tension-filled) view of earthly longevity, aging and death from seven fixed biblical cases, then place the individual profile in a chronological/confessional matrix and compare historically. Treat generalization to Christianity as conditional on coverage; compare motifs, not presumed monolithic systems. Original question: why desire death just when new opportunities for learning, service and witnessing divine acts emerge?
## Phase 1 — enumerate before interpretation
Seed: author-register.json (52 existing corpus entries, 50 named individuals and 2 unresolved collective labels; not necessarily verified relevant authors). Search seven passages and context, not just Luke. Include authors with at least one substantive relevant surviving treatment; do NOT require all seven, which would bias against ancient and medieval writers. Include direct exegesis as core; sermons, treatises, hagiography, pastoral application marked as distinct genres and sensitivity subcorpus, not dropped. Anonymous and collective texts separately identified, attributed only with evidence. No independent Jewish commentary sample; citations in Christian texts only.
Discovery sources: BiblIndex https://www.biblindex.org/en/overview (ancient Greek/Latin biblical citations, bibliographical locators, no full texts); Stegmüller https://tcdh.uni-trier.de/en/projekt/repertorium-biblicum (medieval Latin works through 1500, book-level repertoire); PRDL https://www.prdl.org/ and biblical index https://prdldev.juniusinstitute.org/biblical.php (early modern works, selective); BibleHub https://biblehub.com/commentaries/ (mostly accessible English-language collection, often multi-author and incomplete); Orthodox library https://azbyka.ru/otechnik/ and https://bible.optina.ru/ (Russian translations/works, verify author and edition). Modern bibliographies and catalogues for XIX–XXI, including Catholic and Orthodox sources; document additional Eastern-language coverage or limits, no unapproved expansion of confessional design. Do not treat any index as exhaustive global author census. Search by book, verse, wording variants, liturgical incipits, and references in verified works; enumerate every hit in declared selected catalogues, deduplicate aliases/editions/collections, log per-source inclusion and exclusion. Registry candidate requires identified author, work, approximate date, tradition, edition, passage/locator, text URL if accessible or catalog URL with unavailability, status. BibleHub navigation labels are NOT verified author hits. Distinguish count of candidate persons, confirmed relevant persons, anonymous/collective works, unavailable/unresolved. Freeze v1 roster when all seven passage searches in declared source set are enumerated, duplicates resolved or flagged, and each candidate screened; newly found authors recorded in next roster version, not endless scope creep. Cannot assert completeness for all historical Christianity; report completeness relative to declared sources, languages, dates and search protocol, missing texts.
## Phase 2 — complete author dossiers, one author at a time
For each frozen roster person inspect all seven cases in order: S Simeon Luke 2:25–35; J Jacob Gen 46:30,47:28,48–49; M Moses Deut 3:23–29,31:2,34:5–7; A Anna Luke 2:36–38; P Paul Phil 1:21–26; H Hezekiah 2 Kings 20:1–6/Isa 38; B Barzillai 2 Sam 19:31–39 (numbering varies). Keep seven slots even where no preserved/available text. State: not searched / work found but not checked / text found relevant / text found not relevant / no relevant passage found after stated searches / inaccessible / attribution uncertain. Do not conflate absence, silence, and disagreement. Record all relevant distinct works (one author may change over time), exact quote, translation, locator, text and edition links, genre, source quality; preserve B/Y/G/D and separate motives; distinguish author's own judgment vs character's disposition, desire vs readiness vs predicted death, life utility vs active wish, health, completion of goal, spiritual growth, afterlife, divine will. Note direct use of biblical parallel and cited predecessors. Then draft profile: shared principles, distinctions between cases, apparent tensions, possible reconciliation grounded in text, unresolved contradictions and confidence/coverage. No forced coherence. One completed dossier/checkpoint before next author, except during census. Place chronological by work/date; if different positions by age, retain both time points rather than a single timeless color. Existing Simeon cards and graph preserved; later multi-episode matrix and graph derive from structured records.
## Phase 3 — compare and publish
After frozen roster dossiers, assess patterns within author, era, confession, school, context and genre, controlling citation dependence and missingness; test historical hypotheses including counterexamples. Final product: source register, seven-slot author profiles, chronology, typed graph and synthesis with bounded claims. Report coverage (completed profiles, slots searched, relevant found, inaccessible, uncertain), not just positive results. Completion is census v1 and all its dossiers processed, with unresolved gaps documented. User can approve roster boundary and further expansion after census report.
