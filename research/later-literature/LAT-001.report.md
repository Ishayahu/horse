## Meaningful findings

- Maharal Gevurot47:53: Egypt as horse and its governing prince as rider. A hierarchy allegory, rather than literal celestial horses.
- Gevurot29:4–12: horse appears as a counterfactual alternative within donkey/materiality interpretation. Paragraph8 expressly calls the donkey natural; paragraph12 explains its identity across Abraham/Moses/Messiah by shared rank and function. Do not assert a twilight-created horse from the question in paragraph9.
- Be'er Well6:3:1: horse pulling cart explains a causal relation between star and sphere; no actual cosmic horse.
- Chida Chomat Anakh6:18: fiery protection could be destructive, while Elisha chooses blindness to spare lives and sanctify God's name. No crossing worlds.
- Midbar Kedemot Peh1: body/soul model of Torah meaning, with horse/mule verse and pered/Pardes wordplay. Zayin8: mare/mule names enter a reported kabbalistic letter association; cited Aspaklaria/ Shimshon attribution chain not yet independently read. Mem38: royal-limit mnemonic.
- Rambam I:70: rider/mount explains divine governance and independence from celestial sphere; chariot/four horses supplies an analogy for merkavah nomenclature. II:43 explicitly classes Zechariah's horses among prophetic parables.
