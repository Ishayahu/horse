# KAB-002 — bounded Ari-school contextual selection

Seven named Hebrew passages were read with saved API receipts and bounded context: *Sefer Etz Chaim* 49:7:2; *Pri Etz Chaim, Gate of Songs* 6:8; *Sha'ar Ma'amarei Rashbi, Kedoshim* 1–2; *Sha'ar HaPesukim, Psalms* 58–59; and *Sha'ar Ruach HaKodesh* 129:20. All seven records are present in `KAB-002.sources.jsonl` and the shared registry.

The material yields several distinct uses that must not be conflated:

- body/horse and evil-inclination symbolism;
- horse and rider as Samael and his female partner in a Sea Song interpretation;
- ordinary moral comparison with equine lust and restraint;
- an ordinary Sabbath rider narrative inside a kabbalistic collection;
- soul/body or holiness/weekday symbolic structures.

These are attributed symbolic or moral readings. They do not establish a biological celestial horse, wings, Pegasus identity, or a literal mechanism by which a horse carries a human between worlds. Attribution is also qualified: one passage explicitly says “from the students of the Ari,” and other paragraphs contain redactional or source markers that prevent assigning every sentence directly to the Ari.

This completes the named bounded Ari-school batch. It does not exhaust every Lurianic recension, manuscript, later commentary, spelling variant or related verse. Historical composition/redaction and manuscript chains remain unresolved; no printed/manuscript collation was performed. Selected central records already received independent review in `VER-005k`.
