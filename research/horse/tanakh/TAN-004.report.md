## Acceptance boundaries

Most new accepted records describe military/royal chariots, their officers, cavalry, escape or transport. The category is **indirect association**, never an assertion that every verse literally names a horse. Troop/vehicle counts are not counts of horses. Ezra8:22 records declined cavalry protection, not an escort actually supplied. 1Kgs18:45 is accepted from the neighboring royal transport instruction אסר: the vehicle/animal remains unspecified in the target verse.

**Ps45:5** is retained as possible royal martial riding imagery, because the neighboring sword and arrows supply a military setting. The verse names neither horse nor chariot; the record is explicitly uncertain and should not be counted as horse-confirmed evidence. **Song6:12** names chariots but the sentence and referent remain difficult; no named horse or mystical interpretation is inferred. Both require commentary before stronger conclusions.

**Nah2:4–5** describes the glittering military chariots of an attacking army, comparing them to torches and lightning. It does not establish supernatural or fiery horses. Nah2:14 threatens destruction of Nineveh's chariots. Neither the imagery nor its divine speaker changes the object into a horse.

Rejections retain rather than erase relevant comparative material: 1Kgs7:33 compares temple stand wheels with chariot wheels; Song3:10 describes a royal palanquin seat; 1Chr13:7 places the ark on a cart but does not establish horse traction in the read ±1 range. Neh2:12 has an unspecified riding animal; Ezek27:20 has riding coverings with no named species. None becomes a horse record solely from רכ״ב.

Strongmen, stubborn hearts, divine titles, bulls and sacrificial animals are distinguished from horses. Jer46:15 remains rejected as horse evidence because the adjacent text does not decide between mighty men and a bull-associated reading. Ps76:6's stronghearted warriors remain human despite the next verse naming a horse. Zech9:9 explicitly rides an ass; Zech9:10's chariot and horse contrast does not change that mount. Cloud, wind, heaven and cherub riding (Job30:22; Ps18:11,68:5,68:34,104:3; Isa19:1) is not automatically horse imagery.
