## Parallel comparison and numbering correction

Guggenheimer's editorial locator GenesisRabbah68(10) must not be blindly converted to current Sefaria68:10. Current Sefaria and Wikisource locate horse paragraph at68:9, attributed Rabbi Abba bar Yudan. Its exact wording is “הסוס טפילה לרוכב ואין הרוכב טפילה לסוס” (Wikisource), with Habakkuk3:8 quotation. It therefore states subordination/dependence, not the Yerushalmi's exact wording that hero bears horse. Both occur after place/dwelling teaching; relation is a genuine primary-text parallel with wording/attribution variation, not identical quotation. Verse quote in Sefaria has plural סוסיך; Wikisource prints סוסך. Historical edition numbering and manuscript provenance remain unchecked.
