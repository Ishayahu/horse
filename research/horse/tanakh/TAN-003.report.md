## Context scope and decisions

Actual reviewed remainder: Torah, Joshua and Judges; 1–2 Samuel; 2 Kings; plus **1Chr28:18, Ps68:18, Isa66:15**. The exact ranges are `context_refs` and full original Hebrew `context_quotes` in candidates/source records. Reading normally means verse ±1 within its chapter, not the whole book. 1 Kings, most Chronicles/Writings and the remaining prophets are pending; merely printing portions of an inventory did not grant context-read status. 7409 רכש produces four verses: 1Kgs5:8, Esth8:10,14, Mic1:13, all already covered by TAN-002a/b. Its presence is now reproducibly searched rather than inferred from a סוס-only index.

`TAN-003.decisions.jsonl` is the explicit manually reviewed decision input. `TAN-003.audit.jsonl` gives one status/reason per candidate, old source IDs for overlaps and accepted source IDs for new records. `TAN-003.sources.jsonl` contains **34 unique stable `tanakh-wlc-…-indirect` IDs**, disjoint from previous source IDs. Rejected candidates remain auditable with complete original adjacent text in the candidates file; they are not promoted to sources.

Acceptance distinguishes ordinary military/royal chariots, cavalry and context-dependent transport from explicit horse tokens. A chariot is **horse-related indirect evidence**, not proof that this verse literally names a horse. 2Sam1:6 is a character's report; its historical truth is not inferred from quotation. 2Kgs9:25 does not resolve the vehicle from the word "pairs". 2Kgs9:28 derives transport association from the preceding chariot scene, not an explicit animal in the verse.

Rejected forms include named camels, donkeys and mules; upper millstones; ritual riding seats; a hand placed on a bow; divine riding on heavens or a cherub; אביר as divine title or human chief. **1Chr28:18** describes a golden chariot/cherub design over the ark: no horse established. It remains relevant to comparative merkava inquiry, but is excluded from horse-confirmed evidence.

**Ps68:18 and Isa66:15** are accepted only as *theophanic chariot* records. They do not name horses. Ps68:18 does not establish spiritual horses; Isa66:15 compares God's chariots with a whirlwind, not a literal quotation of fire horses. These central comparative nodes require independent and commentary review. All commentary and independent-review checks remain false.
